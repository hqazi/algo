Pyodide

This application ships Pyodide's runtime files (pyodide.mjs, pyodide.asm.mjs,
pyodide.asm.wasm, python_stdlib.zip, pyodide-lock.json) unmodified, from the
`pyodide` npm package, inside the app's resources (app.asar, renderer/pyodide/).
